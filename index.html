About Me

What do you want to know?

I’m Falisha, a visual designer and illustrator based in Indonesia, specializing in semi-realistic illustration and expressive, story-driven design. My work blends creativity with strategy—transforming visual concepts into compelling narratives that connect emotionally while serving a clear purpose. With a strong focus on composition, color psychology, and visual storytelling, I aim to craft designs that not only captivate the eye but also communicate meaning effectively. I see design as a problem-solving discipline, where each project is an opportunity to balance aesthetics, functionality, and emotion. Whether developing illustrations, brand visuals, or digital art, I’m driven by the challenge of translating ideas into visuals that inspire, engage, and leave a lasting impression.

Working with Citizen OS Indonesia has been an enriching experience. Being part of their graphic design team has given me new perspectives, and I am truly grateful for the opportunity to intern here. I deeply appreciate how Citizen OS consistently upholds and advocates for human rights, which makes this journey even more meaningful.
